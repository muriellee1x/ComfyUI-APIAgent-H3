# Wardrobe Style Rules

Use this global reference whenever a prompt describes visible human clothing, styling, accessories, shoes, armor, fantasy garments, ceremonial costume, or character-sheet wardrobe. It summarizes the W+ wardrobe/accessory knowledge base into prompt-writing rules. Use it as a structure and phrase reference; do not copy long source sentences.

## Wardrobe Design Workflow

1. Choose the silhouette first: suit, gown, robe, uniform, ethnic costume, armor, fantasy creature styling, street outfit, functional outfit, or ceremonial outfit.
2. Add construction details that explain how the garment is built: collar, neckline, waist, sleeve, skirt or trouser shape, hem, panels, straps, layering, fitted waist, corset, robe panels, cape, shoulder structure, or visible support.
3. Add material and placed decoration. Name where decoration sits instead of writing generic quality words. Use forms such as `女孩的裙摆边缘有银色卷草刺绣`, `男孩的西装外套袖口有黑色滚边`, or `神女的腰封中央固定一枚月石扣件`.
4. Add only visible accessories. If the crop is half-body or close-up, focus on hair, collar, shoulder, chest, sleeves, hands, jewelry, and held props; omit shoes and full lower garments unless visible or narratively important.
5. Use footwear only when visible in the framing or required by a full-body asset. If hidden, state a visibility limit such as `鞋履被长裙遮挡不可见`.

## Design Density By Price

- Low price tiers: use a clear silhouette, 1-2 construction details, and a small number of placed decorations. Keep accessories simple.
- Mid price tiers: add layered fabric, stronger waist/shoulder/sleeve construction, visible trim, and one or two refined accessories.
- High price tiers: use ceremonial silhouette, richer layering, premium material contrast, placed embroidery, jewelry chains, crowns, shoulder ornaments, cape or veil structures, and more deliberate shoe or visibility treatment.
- Do not express high value by piling random ornaments. Every added garment detail should clarify role, price tier, theme, or silhouette.

## Construction Vocabulary

Use these as modular phrase sources:

- Collars and necklines: open collar, stand collar, crossed collar, deep V lapel, sweetheart neckline, low round neckline, square neckline, straight strapless neckline, triangular cutout, high-neck capelet.
- Upper-body construction: corset bodice, vertical panel seams, fitted waistline, double-breasted structure, overlapping neckline, diagonal frog closures, dimensional lapel, shoulder panels.
- Waist construction: wide waist cincher, high waistline, hanging waist panels, side satin ribbons, diagonal chains, rose clasp, fish-scale skirt panels, outward metal frame.
- Sleeves: wide sleeves, puff sleeves, lantern sleeves, flared sleeves, lace cuffs, ruffled cuffs, transparent gauze sleeves, satin long sleeves.
- Hem and skirt forms: high-low hem, layered puffy skirt, mermaid hem, floor-length train, slit skirt, robe panels falling to the ankle, skirt expanding below the knee.
- Layering: transparent outer gauze, cape over shoulders and back, short jacket over long shirt, vest over shirt, metal frame over long skirt, gauze over inner skirt.

## Material And Surface

Choose materials that fit the scene, role, and price tier:

- Fabric: satin, brocade, velvet, jacquard, subtle patterned jacquard, coarse-textured fabric, fine pinstripe fabric, glossy fabric, pearlescent material.
- Transparent materials: lace, semi-transparent lace, transparent gauze, iridescent transparent gauze, fish-fin-like translucent panels, semi-transparent wing panels.
- Structured materials: metal breastplate, sequins, silver metal plates, metal relief, leather straps, mesh fabric, ribbed cuffs.
- Thematic textures: fish scales, shell texture, wave pattern, floral damask, cloud pattern, dragon pattern, vine embroidery, starry reflective points.

Prefer stable premium materials such as silk, velvet, pearl, silver, brocade, clear crystal, lace, gauze, and controlled metallic accents. Use laser, holographic, rainbow-reflective, or highly iridescent materials only when the theme explicitly needs them.

## Decoration And Accessories

Use decoration only when it has placement and purpose:

- Embroidery: vine embroidery, floral branch embroidery, dragon embroidery, bamboo leaf embroidery, crane and plum embroidery, gold/silver wave embroidery.
- Edges: gold piping, dark folded edge, pale trim, lace scallop edge, wide ruffle edge.
- Hanging details: pearl chains, bead-chain tassels, long bead tassels, silver chains, watch chain, falling ribbons, red tassels, bead strings.
- Dimensional details: dimensional flowers, floral chest ornament, butterfly metal piece, butterfly applique, shell-shaped panels, feather-leaf metal frame.
- Shine points: sequins, tiny beads, crystal beads, blue gemstone pendant, star-like reflections, metallic gloss, soft satin reflection.
- Hair and head accessories: wide-brim hat, top hat, beret, small hat, flower crown, pearl headband, butterfly hair ornament, branch-and-moon crown, gold/silver floral crown, silver-blue crystal crown, veil.
- Jewelry and body accessories: pearl necklace, layered pearl necklace, lace choker, leather choker, wide neck ring, gemstone necklace, brooch, pocket square, round pocket watch, jade pendant, bracelet, arm ring, long gloves, chain waist accessory, long tassel waist ornament.

## Theme Patterns

- New Chinese style: stand collar, frog closures, crossed robe, jade pendant tassel, cloud or dragon embroidery, gold piping, wide waist cincher.
- Academy style: school blazer, shield badge, knitted vest, striped tie, brown trim, straight trousers.
- Gatsby / retro ball: straight sequin dress, pearl headband, bead-chain tassels, satin long gloves, round-toe heels.
- Xianxia / goddess: crossed robe, floating scarf, metal floral pattern, chain pendant, semi-transparent long gauze, crown, hanging forehead chains.
- Ocean / mermaid: shell bodice, fish-scale skirt panels, coral or starfish accessories, iridescent transparent tail fin, fish-fin earrings.
- Functional techwear: stand-collar zipper, mesh fabric, utility pockets, sleeve badge, studs, paneled trousers, platform sneakers.

## Footwear And Visibility

- Use precise footwear when visible: white lace-up leather shoes, black glossy lace-up leather shoes, brown glossy lace-up leather shoes, two-tone glossy shoes, platform sneakers, tall boots, embroidered boots, metal-edged long boots, armor boots, round-toe heels, pointed heels, strap heels, Mary Jane shoes, embroidered round-toe shoes, sandals.
- Use visibility wording when needed: `鞋尖从长裙下方露出`, `鞋面从流苏裙摆下方露出`, `鞋履被长裙遮挡不可见`, `鞋履不存在，脚部由鱼尾替代`.

## Hard Rules

- Do not write vague conclusions such as `服饰与整体风格一致`, `人物服装与场景主题相匹配`, or `服饰精致华丽` without concrete construction, material, and placed decoration.
- Do not add lower-body garments or shoes in half-body, close-up, waterline, table-occluded, or foreground-occluded compositions unless they are visible.
- Keep decoration placement clear when ambiguity would affect the design, such as sleeve edge, collar, skirt hem, shoulder clasp, or waist clasp. Do not repeat the character name for every clothing detail when the sentence is already clear.
- Keep clothing coverage ordinary and non-exploitative. Do not focus on intimate anatomy.

## Examples

- Full-body girl: `w+girl，人物全身图，年轻女性，银白色长发，表情安静，穿月白色束腰长裙，裙身为缎面与半透明薄纱叠层结构，女孩的心形领口边缘有细银色滚边，女孩的腰封中央固定一枚月石扣件，裙摆外层薄纱垂到脚踝并带银色卷草刺绣，鞋尖从长裙下方露出，双臂向两侧展开，做出标准的A-pose，画面为纯黑色背景，轻微逆光，金色轮廓光。`
- Full-body boy: `w+boy，人物全身图，年轻男性，深棕色短发，表情沉稳，穿墨绿色立领礼服外套，男孩的外套前襟为双排扣结构，肩部有窄金属拼片，袖口有黑色滚边，内搭象牙白竖褶衬衫和深色高腰西装长裤，腰侧垂下一条细金色怀表链，脚穿黑色亮面系带皮鞋，双臂向两侧展开，做出标准的A-pose，画面为纯黑色背景，轻微逆光，金色轮廓光。`
